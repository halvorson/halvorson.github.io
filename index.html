Off the clock, it's the tools below: small, AI-assisted builds that solve exactly one problem for exactly one household.

That household includes a toddler, a dog, and a lot of time outside — running, biking, and backcountry skiing and hiking around the Bay Area. Most of what follows comes out of one of those threads.
